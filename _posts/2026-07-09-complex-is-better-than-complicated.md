Python has the complex data type built-in and handles complex arithmetic easily.  

I worked with several languages before python and most required some hack or additional fudging to do this, and none (other than good old Fortran) did it simply.  Now one can always use the extendabiliy of a language to implement complex numbers, but that just generates a raft of different implementations and many incompatibilties.  Having it built in keeps is elegant and simple.

Then add in the bonus of the fantastic libraries such as scipy, numpy etc and it is as they say a 'no brainer'.  Complex numbers are a vital mathematical tool and being able to handle basic arithmetic, solve equations, plot data and many more evertday tasks for an electrical (an other) engineers is not a luxury, but an essential.
