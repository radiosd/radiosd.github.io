Planning is starting for lectures in 2027.  The next course will be __The Essentials of Radio Communications__  with the venue TBD.

This is a comprehensive series of lectures (3 days) that condenses years of industrial experience in radio systems into managable 
topics that cover all the important aspects.  There is a  course [summary](/pages/EoRadComm)  and for those interested in more, 
look at the CEI-Europe [website](https://www.cei.se/course-026-essentials-of-radio-communications-systems-group.html) for details, 
time, place, cost etc.
