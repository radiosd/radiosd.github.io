The brilliant DSP Related website has recently launched a wonderful initiative, [DSP Foundations](/pages/DSP_Essentials).

The DSP Related [web page](https://www.dsprelated.com/foundations.php) is the portal to the various topics.  Each one contains several separate bite sized lessons that describe thes fundamental concepts, provide examples and valuable insight into them from recognised industrial experts.  For both the newcommer and the old hand, this is a first class resource to learn and/or revisit key ideas.
