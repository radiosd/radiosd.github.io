I was pleased to attend EuMW in London for the first time in several years.  The Excel Centre is as busy as ever, and the Elizabeth Line makes it more acceesible both from central London and Heathrow.  Located in the heart of the old east end docks, it us still not quite what most people would consider to be "in London".

Even so, it was great to see old friends, colleagues and other associates.  A perfect opportunity to catch up on technical issues and new product offerings at the exhibition.
