New Hands on course for Software Defined Radio.

If you are like me and SDR was not even invented when you were in school then this course might be of interest.  On the other hand if you find the rather abstract theory of DSP hard to follow, this may also be of interest.  

__Feb__: Introductory Hands On SDR Course - A practical introduction to SDR for harware 
engineers.  Thanks to the staff at Rohde and Schwarz in the UK for giving me the opportunity to run this couse for the first time.

Students bring there own laptops to the course, boot from the Ubuntu live USB drive provided
and then have access to all the software they need.  A Pluto SDR is provided 
for their use during the course, providing a complete SDR development platform to learn from.
