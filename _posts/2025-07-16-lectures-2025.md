Planning started for 2026.  The next course will be __The Essentials of Radio Communications__ in Barcelona, April 13 - 15.

This is a comprehensive series of lectures (3 days) that condenses years of industrial experience in radio systems into managable topics that cover all the important aspects.  There is a  course [summary](/pages/EoRadComm)  and for those interested in more  look at the CEI-Europe [website](https://www.cei.se/course-026-essentials-of-radio-communications-systems-group.html) for details, time, place, cost etc.
